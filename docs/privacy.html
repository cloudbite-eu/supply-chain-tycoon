Supply Chain Tycoon

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Supply Chain Tycoon (the app) is made by Lukasz Abramowicz (“we”), who is responsible for the data described here. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the app on iPhone or iPad. In short: there is no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. To let you play with other players, the app uses an anonymous game account that holds only what the game needs.

What stays on your device

Your saved games, settings and the company name you type are stored on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Playing with others

The weekly ladder with other players, tenders against their companies and trade associations run on our game server. The first time the app starts, it creates an anonymous account there: a random player number and a secret key, kept on your device. The account is not linked to your name, email address, Apple ID, contacts or device. The server does not store IP addresses.

With that account the server keeps:

  • your company's name and colours and your game results: the ladder, the hall of fame, daily challenges and tender bids;
  • while the app is open, which countries you play in, so players in the same markets see each other (kept for 24 hours);
  • your trade association (its name, tag and motto if you founded it) and the last 30 chat lines you wrote there;
  • chat lines you report, with the reason, until we have dealt with them (at most 90 days after);
  • a backup copy of your live empire, which only your account can read.

Company names, results, association names and chat lines are shown to other players, so treat them as public and do not write anything personal. Chat lines and names pass a filter for insults, links and phone numbers. We read reported content and may suspend accounts that break the association rules.

We use this data only to run the game and keep it safe for players: to provide the game you asked for, and our legitimate interest in moderating it (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) of the GDPR). We do not sell it, share it for advertising or use it to track you. The server runs on hosting we rent; the hosting company processes the data only on our behalf.

Accounts that are not used for 12 months are deleted with everything they wrote.

Deleting your online data

In the app, open the menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose Delete my online data under Your online data. This deletes your account and everything it wrote from the server at once. The menu also shows your player number; quote it if you write to us about your data.

The daily world file

Every few hours, while the app is open, it downloads a public file with the day's world facts (freight rates, oil prices, shipping routes, trade rules) from GitHub Pages. The request carries no information about you or your game. As with any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic technical information to deliver the file; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or store this information.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards under your Game Center profile. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, your email address or your contacts. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings at any time.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules reminders on your device, for example when a decision is near its deadline. They are created on the device and nothing is sent to us.

Links

News items show their sources. Tapping a source opens it in Safari, where the website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not made for children. We do not knowingly hold data about children; the online account is anonymous and asks for no personal details.

Your rights

You can delete your online data in the app at any time. You may also ask us what we hold about your account, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use: write to hello@cloudbite.eu with your player number. If you live in the European Union, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
