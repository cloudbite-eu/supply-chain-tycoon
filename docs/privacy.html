Supply Chain Tycoon

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Supply Chain Tycoon (the app) is made by Lukasz Abramowicz (“we”), who is responsible for the data described here. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the app on iPhone or iPad. In short: there is no sign-up, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and this version of the app collects no data about you.

What stays on your device

Your saved games, settings and the company name you type are stored on your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Playing with others

This version of the app plays on its own: there is no online account and no game server. If a later version adds playing with other players, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

The daily world file

Every few hours, while the app is open, it downloads a public file with the day's world facts (freight rates, oil prices, shipping routes, trade rules) from GitHub Pages. The request carries no information about you or your game. As with any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address and basic technical information to deliver the file; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or store this information.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards under your Game Center profile. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, your email address or your contacts. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings at any time.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules reminders on your device, for example when a decision is near its deadline. They are created on the device and nothing is sent to us.

Links

News items show their sources. Tapping a source opens it in Safari, where the website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not made for children. It collects no data about anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data about you. For any question about privacy, write to hello@cloudbite.eu. If you live in the European Union, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
