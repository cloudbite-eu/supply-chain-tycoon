Supply Chain Tycoon

Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea for the game? Write to hello@cloudbite.eu. Please say which device you use and, for a bug, what you did just before it happened.

Where does the real-world news come from?

Once a day a new snapshot of the world is published: freight rates, oil prices, the euro, the state of shipping routes and trade rules, each with its source. The game checks for it every few hours while it is open. Events after today are scenarios built on those facts; they are not predictions.

Where is my game saved?

On your device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

Can I play with other players?

Not yet: this version plays on its own, against three rival companies run by the computer. Playing with others is planned for a later version.

How do I start again?

Open the menu (the three dots at the top right), choose Lobby and start a new company there. Your career, people and rewards stay.

What does my chief operating officer do?

In your live empire you can hire a chief operating officer (the button at the top right). At the end of each day they take the decisions you left, in the management style you pick, as far as their skill and character reach, and log each one with its reason; what they do not get to waits for its deadline. They are paid like the rest of your team. You can send them off duty, take back any area or replace them at any time. The funds have a free autopilot that takes every call you leave to it.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Game Center. If you are not signed in to Game Center on your device, the game works the same; your scores just stay on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders (in Your day or in the menu), the game reminds you of meetings when they open, decisions near their deadline and builds that finish: at most three a day, the ones close together in one, and never between 21:00 and 8:00. They are scheduled on your device. Turn them off in the menu, or in Settings, Notifications, SC Tycoon.
