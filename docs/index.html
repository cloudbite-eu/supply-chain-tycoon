Supply Chain Tycoon

Run a supply chain in a world that follows the news

Take over an importer of spare parts for electric cars: body and lighting parts, power electronics, battery modules and heat pumps. The parts are made in Asia and sold to repair workshops, body shops and fleets across Europe. Plan the stock, choose the routes, handle the morning calls and outgrow three rival distributors.

Every day the game's world updates from real events: container freight rates, the price of oil, the euro, canal and strait closures, port strikes and trade rules, each with its source.

For iPhone and iPad. Free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
